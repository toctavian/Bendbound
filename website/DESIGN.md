# Bendbound promotional website

This is a one-page marketing extension of the native app's established identity.
The app's root DESIGN.md remains authoritative for the app itself.

## Direction

A sentence-led opening with a real route preview embedded beside the title,
followed by the actual app at phone scale. Taste direction: **The sentence is the
layout**, seed **289185989**. The screenshot tour is the live interaction: choose
planning, recording or saved tours to see the corresponding app screen and copy.
The user requested a simple page, so there are no scroll scenes, looping video,
new artwork, stock scenery, testimonial sections or invented popularity claims.

## Tokens

Canvas `#111214`, surface `#1a1b1e`, raised `#292b2f`, ink `#f5f6f7`, secondary
`#9b9ea4`, route red `#ec3347`, action cyan `#149ed0`; logo/header/footer black.
The unchanged approved logo is `public/assets/bendbound-logo.png`.
Keep the app's platform sans family. Desktop source uses five type sizes: 14/16/18
for notes, controls and body; responsive section and hero titles up to 72/96px.
Main page gutters are 32px desktop and 20px phone; content max-width 1280px.
Use flat tone changes, pill actions, a 30px screenshot crop and no resting shadows.

## Accessibility and states

Semantic page headings and navigation, skip link, visible cyan focus, meaningful
screenshot alt text, 44px-plus controls, arrow/Home/End tab navigation and proper
ARIA selection. Image/copy updates are actual feature previews, not a simulated
routing service. Honour reduced-motion preferences and keep content readable
without scripts. Public downloads are explicitly unavailable until a real link
is supplied.

## Evidence

Browser inspector at 1440, 768 and 390px: 0 FAIL. `few-pictures` is intentionally
allowed: the page demonstrates a real app with three screenshots, rather than
adding a fourth unrelated stock picture. Video and extra-motion warnings are
intentional for this short page. Independent reviewer inspected all six final
screenshots and returned **ship**. Keyboard feature navigation was exercised.
