Some days you have a destination. Some days you just have time for a ride. Bendbound makes room for both.

A destination. Or a round trip.

Add your stops or choose a distance and direction. Pick fast, winding or twisty roads, with motorway avoidance when you want it.

A look inside the current app. Example routes shown.

Keep the
ride.

Record the GPS trail you actually ride, with distance and riding time. Find it again in your tours, alongside the routes you’ve saved for another day.

Already have a route? Bring it along as a GPX file. Take your recorded tracks with you when you want to share them.
