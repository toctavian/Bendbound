Before you ride.

Can I download Bendbound?

Bendbound is in early beta for iPhone. Public App Store downloads and TestFlight invitations aren’t open yet. Follow the project on GitHub for development updates.

Can I plan a motorcycle round trip?

Yes. Choose a distance and direction, or plan a route with stops. Pick fast, winding or twisty roads and avoid motorways when you want to.

Does Bendbound work with GPX?

You can import a GPX route and export recorded tracks. Bendbound also records your GPS trail, distance and riding time, and keeps saved routes and recorded rides in your tours.

Is there an Android version?

Bendbound is currently an iPhone app in early beta. An Android release isn’t available.
